Rules applied: no RAD, no new per-sortie limits, no new tokens/counters/stat fields, single-value shield DR, no melee multipliers,
divisor ladder (3)-(6), TL bands (TL8 = match core, TL9 0..+2, TL10 +1..+3), guns on the lower range scale, HEAT moderate, Reman has no drawbacks.
Factions as written in the catalogue: Core Empire = Imperial, Crow Republic = Crow, Reman.

== GAP ANALYSIS ==
- Artillery: one indirect weapon (105mm Siege Mortar) and nothing that improves spotting or indirect fire; the INDIRECT rule exists but has no support kit around it.
- Offensive EW: ECM Suite, Countermeasure Pod and Decoy Beacon are all area or self defense. Nothing lets an EW pilot shut down one enemy's fire control.
- Counter-stealth: Shinobi dampers, Kitsune Mirage and Smoke exist, but only Sensor Suite tiers answer them. No hunter item.
- Anti-air/interceptor: fighters, helos, Tengu, jump jets, Icarus Wings and Spaceborn jets exist, but no weapon is tuned to hit airborne units.
- Escort: every shield and defense protects only its bearer (the Convoy harness is movement only). Nothing lets a guard take hits for an ally.
- Logistics: no way to rearm or cool an ally in the field. Ammo and heat are the two resources that end sorties.
- Rescue: the Tow Winch and Armored Carrier move wrecks and cargo. Nothing secures a downed pilot or gets a knocked-down or stunned ally back up.
- Command: the Heraldic Command Mast is the only command item, and it only adds to-hit. Nothing gives allies movement or tempo.
- Breacher vs shield walls: the Pile Bunker and Breaching Torch hit hard, but Tower/Large shields and Aura-Guard blades still block them. Nothing answers a shield line.
